FAQ

How much does a ride cost?

At standard times a ride costs €5.60 per 30 minutes (€2.80 per 15 minutes). In July and August, for rides starting between 10:00 and 16:00, it costs €8.50 per 30 minutes. Rides are billed per minute: a 10-minute ride costs €1.87 at standard times and €2.83 in the peak window.

Why do rides cost more in July and August from 10:00 to 16:00?

In this window the bikes are in very high demand and our fleet is limited. The window is fixed and the same for everyone. The price is set when you unlock the bike and stays the same for the whole ride: a ride that starts at 9:59 or at 16:00 costs the Standard Ride price, a ride that starts between 10:00 and 15:59 the Summer Peak Ride price.

What do the passes include?

The Monthly Pass (€24.99 per month) and the Student Pass (€12.99 per month) include unlimited rides of up to 30 minutes each. The Monthly Pass is not valid in the peak window: a ride that starts then is charged at the Summer Peak Ride price.

Who can get the Student Pass?

Students with a valid e-student ID, which is confirmed in the app. The Student Pass is available from October to June.
